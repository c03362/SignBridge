Recording guidance

  • Use a different signer ID for each person.
  • Record each number several times, not just repeatedly from one pose.
  • Vary distance, lighting, background and camera angle.
  • For two-handed signs, make sure both hands are visible.
  • Do not guess a sign. Confirm its correct form with a fluent signer or trusted language resource.
  • Only record people who have agreed to contribute samples.

This tool saves hand landmark coordinates, handedness and labels. It does not upload or save webcam video.
