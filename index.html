About SignBridge

Our goal is to develop an accessible communication platform supporting multiple sign languages and generic finger counting.

📷

Live hand tracking

Browser-based hand tracking with 21 landmarks per detected hand.

🌐

Multiple languages

Separate recognition pathways are needed for ISL, ASL, BSL and CSL.

🤖

AI assistance

Ask questions and request explanations of supplied recognition results.

Privacy and camera information

Camera video is used by MediaPipe in your browser for live hand tracking. When you request OpenHands translation, a short recorded video clip is uploaded to the SignBridge backend for processing. The recognition model is not currently deployed on the public backend, so ISL recognition may be unavailable. The camera is not continuously streamed to the backend.

Do not record or submit another person's signs without their knowledge and consent. Dataset recordings should use an appropriate consent process.
