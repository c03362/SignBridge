About SignBridge

Our goal is to develop an accessible communication platform supporting multiple sign languages and generic finger counting.

Privacy and camera information

Camera video is used by MediaPipe in your browser for live hand tracking. When you request OpenHands translation, a short recorded video clip is uploaded to the SignBridge backend for processing. The OpenHands ISL model is hosted on a separate public backend. Short recorded video clips are uploaded for experimental isolated-sign recognition. Recognition availability depends on the hosting service. The camera is not continuously streamed to the backend.

Do not record or submit another person's signs without their knowledge and consent. Dataset recordings should use an appropriate consent process.
